- Three-frame strips are padded to four frames by holding frame 3 at build time (original copied PNGs remain unchanged). All pets are drawn at integer multiples of the native 32px grid. Cosmetic layer size and position follow the manifest, rounded to native-grid pixels before integer upscaling.
- Dummy slates, abstract weather map, broadcast typography, diagrams, CRT/VHS shaders and sound effects are drawn/synthesized in code. No generated image service is used.
- Bundled broadcast fonts and licenses are documented below.
